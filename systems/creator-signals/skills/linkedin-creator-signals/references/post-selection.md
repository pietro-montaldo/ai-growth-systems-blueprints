# Post selection

Only posts about something you sell are worth scraping for comments. Read every post and decide **keep**, **drop** or **unsure**. Record a short reason that starts with the rule, and a topic of two or three lowercase words about what the post is really about ("claude skills", "ai outbound"). The topic feeds the Interests column and the drafts.

{{KEEP_TOPICS}}

## Drop

- **Personal:** life news, family, travel, health, celebrations, gratitude, opinions with no work angle.
- **Hiring or job news:** "we're hiring", "I'm starting a new position".
- **Promo with no content:** an event, webinar or launch with nothing to learn.
- **Competing offer:** the creator selling what you sell. The commenters are being sold the same thing; you don't pitch in someone else's sales thread.
- **Too far from you:** news, funding rounds, policy, anything your buyers don't act on.
{{DROP_TOPICS}}

## Unsure

A general post on your theme with no clear link to an offer. Mark it unsure with reason "general". It is scraped with the kept posts but listed apart at Pause 1, so you can switch it off.

## Lead magnet posts

"Comment GUIDE and I'll send it": most comments will be one word. Keep the post if the topic fits. The few real comments under it are often good, and screening drops the keyword ones.
