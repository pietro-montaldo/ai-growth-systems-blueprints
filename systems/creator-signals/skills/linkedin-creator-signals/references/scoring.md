# Scoring people against the ICP

Score from the **live ICP page**, read at the start of every run. Use only the cards of {{OFFERS_IN_SCOPE}}. Never copy the cards here.

## Inputs per person

- **Clay "Enrich Person":** current company, title, start date of the role, company size and industry, location, summary.
- **Apify:** headline, their comment, the creator and post topic.
- **Interests:** one short line from the post topics they chose, what their comment says they care about, and their summary. Example: "automating client reporting; formatting keeps breaking".

When the headline and Clay disagree, trust Clay's current role and say so in Why. Stale headlines are common.

## Steps

1. **Pick the card** that fits best across the offers in scope, from each card's "who" and "find them". Mention a second fit in Why.
2. **Start from the card's fit** (5 to 2, as the card shows).
3. **Adjust with the card's score points** the data supports: geography (score, not filter), a real comment on an on-topic post, company size, being self-employed, and minus points the card lists. Keep the card's fit when the points are neutral. Move one step for a clear plus or minus of 3 or more. Never above 5.
4. **Check "not them"** on the card and the hard caps below.
5. **Write Why** in one line: card, the facts that decided it, any doubt. Example: "card 1, independent expert: solo fractional CMO, UK, 3 years in role, asked where to start."

## Hard caps

- **Competitor:** their company sells {{COMPETITOR_SERVICES}}: fit 2 at most, Why starts with "competitor".
- **Wrong layer:** a role your offer doesn't serve (for example a developer, if you teach non-technical people): fit 1.
- **Too junior or no budget:** student, intern, junior role with no business of their own: fit 2 at most.
- **Already a customer or lead:** remove them and say so.

## Tenure

Under 6 months in a role is a trigger on many cards: say so in Why. A founder a year or more into their own business is more likely to have budget than one two months in.

## Shortlist

Fit 4 and 5 go to the channel you pick. Fit 3 stays in the sheet with Why, so you can promote someone by hand.
