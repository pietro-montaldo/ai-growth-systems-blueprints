# Copy rules (shared by the LinkedIn and email skills)

The skills draft. You decide what goes out: LinkedIn messages you send by hand, and an email campaign starts only when you press Launch in Clay.

## 1. Read what exists before asking

- The **messaging page** in your GTM context: value props per offer, words to use and avoid.
- The person's **ICP card**: "lead with" and "say this".
- Your **voice skill** (`<name>-voice`) if installed. Otherwise ask for 2 or 3 messages you wrote that got replies.
- **Saved campaigns** in `settings.md`. If one fits this week's offer, show it and ask "Same goal, CTA and version as last time?" instead of asking from scratch.

## 2. Ask only the gaps, once

1. **Goal:** what a good result looks like (a reply, a sign-up, a call).
2. **CTA:** the one thing you ask for, with the link. Never invent a date, a price or what something covers. Fetch the page if the CTA is a page.
3. **Templates that worked:** 1 to 3 past messages, and how they did if you know.

Save the answers as a row in "Saved campaigns" in `settings.md`.

## 3. Improve your template, then agree it

Your template is the starting point. Improving it means keeping your words, not rewriting them in a new style.

1. Offer 2 or 3 versions that stay close to your sentence. Personalize with the first name, the creator and what the post was about, and the CTA's real topic and date. Fix only what doesn't work (a typo, a vague "on this", the length).
2. Be direct. No reactions to or quotes from their comment ("loved your point on..."), no praise, no clever lines. They read as automated.
3. Show each version filled in for 2 real people from the list, with the length, recommend one, and ask.
4. Draft the batch only with the version you pick or edit. Save it to the campaign.

## 4. When the CTA has options

If you offer several things (sessions, guides, products), list the live ones with title, date and link, then pick one per person from their card, role and interests. Write "<title> (<date>)" in the CTA column. Say the topic in plain words in the message ("a session on automating client reports"). Use short links on LinkedIn so the note stays under the limit.

## Rules for every message

- Keep the shape and length of the agreed version. Personalize only the facts it leaves open.
- Never use the banned phrases in `settings.md`. No em dashes or double dashes.
- Never mention that they were scraped, scored or enriched.
- First names only.
- Check every draft before you show it: length, banned phrases, dashes, and placeholders left unfilled. Fix every one.
