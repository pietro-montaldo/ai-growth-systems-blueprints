# What you do yourself and what goes to the person

If a change is not clearly on the "do it" side, it becomes a numbered decision.

| Change | Do it yourself when | Otherwise, a decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create a deal** | A clear buying ask from a real person or company, it counts as an opportunity (settings), and no existing row matches after searching every table by email, LinkedIn, name and company. Stage: new interest, in conversation, or call booked, from the evidence. | It might be a partnership or a free ask; you can't tell if they would pay; a possible duplicate; unclear which table or product. |
| **Update fields** | Always, when the thread shows it: last contact, latest communication (what was said, who wrote last), next step (with a date when there is one), value (only as they or the person stated it, with currency), and email, LinkedIn or company when empty. Add durable facts to notes; keep what is there. | The match itself is uncertain. A value that conflicts with the one in the row. |
| **Move a stage forward** | The thread shows it: real back and forth, a booking, a proposal sent, terms being discussed, a request to wait. | Any move backwards. A move on a hunch. A skipped stage with no evidence. |
| **Won** | Proof of payment or signature (see `buyer-sweep.md`). | A promise to buy, "we'll sign next week", a code sent, an enrollment or payment you can't match to a row. |
| **Lost** | Only an explicit written no: "we'll pass", "not moving forward", "we chose another provider". | Everything else: no answer, gone quiet, "maybe next year". Propose it with the evidence. |
| **Reopen a lost deal** | They came back with a new, explicit buying ask: move to in conversation and say why. | They came back without a buying ask. |
| **Payment from a client already won** | A recurring payment (retainer, subscription, instalment) from a row already at won: no change except last contact. List it under "Done" as "recurring payment". | A payment you can't match, or a different amount than agreed. |
| **Won client asks for something new** | One row per deal, not per company: a clear ask for a different offer gets its own new row (stage from the evidence), and a line in the old row's notes pointing to it. | It's unclear whether it's a new deal or part of the current one. |
| **Move to another table** | Never on your own, unless settings say when (for example a course buyer asking for team training gets a new row in the company table). | Anything else. |
| **Rows that shouldn't be there** | Never remove them. | Propose removing, with the reason. |
| **Drafts** | Never on your own in a scheduled run. | Offer them: "Reply to Jane (pricing for 30 people)". |

Record every action under "Done" with its evidence in a few words.

## Writing to the CRM

- Search every table before every create. A duplicate is the same person or company asking about the same offer: update it instead. A won client asking for a different offer is a new deal (see above).
- A spreadsheet or CSV: back it up first (`<name>.backup-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` next to it), then write, then read it back to check.
- More than about 20 writes: hand them to a subagent with exact values, then check with a query.
- Dates as ISO dates. LinkedIn links clean, without tracking parameters.
